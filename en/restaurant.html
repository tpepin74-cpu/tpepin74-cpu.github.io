01Chatelay · Val d’Amour
Organic market gardening

Les Jardins de l’Apprenti’Sage

Mickael Merone

In Chatelay, in the Val d’Amour, Mickael Merone and his partner grow organic heritage vegetables and aromatic herbs. Les Jardins de l’Apprenti’Sage has supplied the Castel’s kitchen since 2017.

Working together since 2017

02Bresse poulardes
Bresse poulardes

Rachel Voisard

Rachel Voisard’s Bresse poulardes feature on the menu throughout the year, a product that reflects the character of the house.

Rachel Voisard has supplied the house with Bresse poulardes for over ten years. Boned and prepared as a ballotine, the poularde with vin jaune and morels continues a tradition of more than forty years.

Full information about the house
Gourmet restaurant near Arbois, in the Jura The culinary spirit of the restaurant Le Castel in Les Planches-près-Arbois Le Castel restaurant is classified as a Table Distinguée by the “Logis d'Exception” restaurant guide. SPECIAL EVENINGS: Coming soon: 80s dance night on 30 October 2026, Saint Hubert evening on 20 November 2026, and an 80s dance night at 140 euros per guest for New Year's Eve on 31 December 2026. Game returns in February 2027 (date to be announced). Remember to book your table by telephone on 03.84.66.08.17 or by email at hotel@casteldamandre.com. Every day, the chef and his team offer cuisine made from fresh produce, as organic and local as possible. Everything is homemade. We offer a small seasonal menu shaped by growers and farmers. This is why you will not find strawberry sorbet in December or pumpkin velouté in July. Bresse poularde with vin jaune and morels has been a house tradition for over 35 years, refined over time as cuisine evolves. Fully deboned and cooked as a ballotine, it is prepared for your enjoyment. All our ingredients are prepared on site in our kitchen, without any semi-prepared products. This is why you will find: - Mr Roland GAILLARD, the village fish farmer, for his quality trout - Mrs Rachel VOISARD in ChappelleVoland for BRESSE poulardes and pullets - Les Jardins de l'Apprenti'Sage in Chatlay for vegetables and aromatic herbs We offer a market menu at 59 euros, a vegetarian menu, Menu Castel at 89 euros and à la carte choices, except on special days with a special celebration menu. From May until 30 September, Wednesday evenings have one set menu at 59 euros, by reservation only. We open every lunchtime from 1 May until 30 September, with a bistro set menu at 27 euros and a small à la carte selection (sharing boards, trout au bleu, main-course salads…). Service is on the terrace or in the waterfall dining room in poor weather, except public holidays: a single set menu is offered for Easter and Mother's Day.
