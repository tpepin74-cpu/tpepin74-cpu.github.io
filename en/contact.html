Getting to the Castel

By car

From Paris: A36 towards Dole, then A39, exit 7 Poligny / Bersaillin, towards Arbois and Les Planches-près-Arbois. From Lyon: A39 towards Bourg-en-Bresse, exit 7 Poligny. From Geneva: towards Lausanne, Col de la Givrine, Morez, Champagnole and Arbois.

By train

Arbois station: 5 km. Mouchard TGV station: 15 km. Dole station: 41 km. Besançon station: 54 km. Distances provided by the hotel.

By air

Airports: Dole-Tavaux (40 km), Geneva-Cointrin (107 km), Lyon-Saint-Exupéry (183 km), Basel-Mulhouse (216 km). Indicative distances published by the hotel.
