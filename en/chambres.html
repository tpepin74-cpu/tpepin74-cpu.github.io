Full information about the house
A charming hotel in the Jura Discover our rooms' services and amenities in Les Planches Près Arbois Le Castel Damandre is a charming 4-star hotel in Arbois, in the Jura, open every day. The property offers 14 rooms, including one accessible room, and 4 suites Jacuzzi , with comfortable bedding, harmonious and refined décor, a TV with 40 satellite channels including Canal+, a minibar and a hospitality tray. The restaurant serves lunch and dinner from 1 May to 30 September. From 1 October to 30 April it opens for dinner except on Wednesday evenings, and also serves Sunday lunch. Annual closure from 13 December to 26 December 2026, from 3 January to 21 January 2027 inclusive, and from 12 December 2027 to 26 December 2027 inclusive. The rooms and Jacuzzi suites at this charming Jura hotel are luxurious and modern. Two public electric vehicle charging points (Zephyre) are available in our private car park, at guests' expense. Enclosed private car park with CCTV. Exposed wooden ceiling beams and the colours of the bedding and furniture create a warm atmosphere. Some rooms and suites have a balcony overlooking the waterfall or the Cirque du Fer à Cheval.
