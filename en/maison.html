A few kilometres from Arbois, our 4-star hotel nestles in the pastoral setting of Les Planches-près-Arbois. A house where contemporary décor meets the character of a 16th-century tower.

Over the years, the house has developed its welcome with constant attention to comfort. Eleven rooms in the tower were notably renovated with ERDF support.

The Cuisance · A family story

Through the water.
Through the generations.

Before welcoming travellers, this place already lived to the rhythm of the river. The Cuisance connects its craft heritage with its spirit of hospitality.

  1. 15th–19th centuries

    The river, the first source of power.

    The earliest traces of the site date to the 15th century. The old mill is thought to have produced walnut oil before becoming a paper mill in the 19th century.

  2. 1975

    The beginning of a house.

    Jean-Claude and Michelle Delavenne imagine a hotel and restaurant in the old mill. Le Moulin de la Mère Michelle is born.

  3. 2001

    A story passed down.

    According to Hebdo39's account, Pascal Mathieu takes over his father-in-law's establishment in 2001. The house becomes Castel Damandre. His sons now share in this family adventure.

Historical milestones based on Odile Rigaud's article, published in Hebdo39 on 20 August 2026. Read the full story on Hebdo39
