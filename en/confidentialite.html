A - Depending on the purpose of collection

The personal data we collect varies according to the purpose of collection and the service we provide to you.

In general, we may collect the following categories of personal data directly:

a) personal contact details, such as your surname, first name, email address, postal address and telephone number(s);

3 - How is personal data collected?

A - Ways in which your data is collected

During our relationship, you may provide us with personal data in various ways, particularly on our website, mobile website, online shop and mobile applications: when browsing, purchasing or booking, completing collection forms, subscribing to our e-newsletter, creating an account, applying for a job, posting comments on our social media pages, contacting our establishment, or otherwise sending us your personal data.

B - IP address

An IP address is a series of numbers automatically assigned to your computer by your internet service provider whenever you connect. Our servers record your visit each time you make a request on the website. We collect IP addresses to analyse website trends. We do not transmit information that identifies you: your session is recorded but remains anonymous to us. We may use your IP address in cooperation with your internet service provider to identify you if we need to enforce the terms of use to protect our services or customers, or comply with applicable law.

C - Cookies

To continually improve the quality of services on our website, mobile website, online shop and mobile application and align them with your expectations, EURL MATHIEU RESTAURANT may use cookies, text files that identify your device when you connect to a service. Storing a cookie or tracker on your computer, tablet, smartphone or other device allows EURL MATHIEU RESTAURANT to collect information and personal data, depending on your device settings.

Types of cookies used:

a) Cookies essential to using the website. These let you use its main functions, such as purchasing or booking services. Disabling cookies prevents you from using these functions. The information stored cannot be used for commercial purposes.

b) Website performance cookies. These help us optimise the website and detect any technical issues you encounter. They collect information about how you use the website, including visits per page, error messages displayed, time spent on a page and clicks on particular areas. The information stored cannot be used for commercial purposes.

c) Functional cookies. These let the website remember your choices, such as username, language or region, and offer more precise, personalised features. They can also remember changes made by users, such as text size or font, and provide services you request, such as watching a video or commenting on a blog. The information collected may be anonymised and cannot track your browsing activity on other websites. By using our website, you agree to receive this type of cookie on your device.

d) Advertising and marketing cookies. These collect information about your browsing habits to show advertisements suited to your interests. They also limit how often you see an advertisement and help us assess the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. They are generally installed by advertising networks with the website operator's permission. They remember visits to a website and share that information with other companies, such as advertisers. Targeting or advertising cookies are often linked to website functions supplied by a partner company. Information is collected and shared with third parties to provide audience targeting and optimisation solutions for advertisers and publishers. No personal data is collected and individuals cannot be identified from the data collected.

e) Cookies from third-party websites and social networks. Our website contains links and/or content pointing to other websites and social networks, and we do not know which cookies they use. Please consult the information those third parties and social networks provide about their own cookies.

D - Respect for your privacy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and follow these 2 principles.

- We do not associate cookies with information that directly and personally identifies you. Cookies do not reveal your address, date of birth, telephone number or any other identifying information.

- We do not provide personal information to advertisers or third-party websites displaying advertisements based on your interests.

If several people use your computer or mobile device, or it has several browsers, content shown to other users may reflect your preferences. You can change your browser settings if you wish.

For more information about cookies and targeted advertising, visit the CNIL website: https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-les-outils-pour-les-maitriser.
